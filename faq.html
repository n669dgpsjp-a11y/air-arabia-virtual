Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Air Arabia Virtual, upcoming operations, flights and opportunities to join the team.

QWhat is Air Arabia?
Air Arabia Virtual is a virtual airline recreation on Roblox inspired by the real-world airline. Our primary goal is to create an enjoyable and immersive aviation experience where members can experience a new way of ro-aviation.
QIs Air Arabia operational?
Air Arabia Virtual is currently in development as we work towards building and preparing our facilities for launch. Updates regarding our progress and future operations will be shared through our official community communications.
QHow can I join flights?
Information about participating in Air Arabia Virtual flights will be made available through our official channels as flight operations become available. Upcoming services will also appear on our Scheduled Departures page when published.
QWhere can I find upcoming flights?
Upcoming flight information and schedules will be shared through our official community channels once operations begin. Published services can also be viewed on the Scheduled Departures page.
QHow can I become personnel?
Opportunities to join the Air Arabia Virtual team will be announced when recruitment becomes available. Current opportunities can be viewed on our Careers page, where each vacancy includes its own job description and application information.
QWhat are the personnel requirements?
Requirements may differ depending on the position being applied for. Full requirements and role-specific information will be provided alongside each recruitment opportunity.

Still got a question?

If your question has not been answered here, our team can help. This FAQ may be updated as new services, opportunities and information become available.
